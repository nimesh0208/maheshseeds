Kya aap gaon tak delivery karte hain?

Haan. Madhya Pradesh ke zyadatar pincodes par 2–5 din me delivery hoti hai. Baaki rajyon me 4–7 din lag sakte hain. Order ke baad hum phone par pincode confirm karte hain.

Beej ki quality kaise check hoti hai?

Hum sirf certified lots kharidte hain aur har lot ka germination test report lete hain. Packet par batch number aur packing date hoti hai. Koi bhi doubt ho to WhatsApp par batch number bhejein — hum report share kar dete hain.

Pesticide kharidne ke liye koi licence chahiye?

Kisan ko personal use ke liye licence ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Humare saare pesticides licensed manufacturers se aate hain aur label par dose, waiting period aur safety instructions likhi hoti hain. BT cotton beej sirf licensed dealer se milte hain.

Payment kaise hota hai?

Abhi Cash on Delivery aur UPI-on-delivery available hai. Online card payment gateway par kaam chal raha hai. Is site par koi card ya UPI PIN nahi maanga jaata.

Product kharab mile to kya karein?

Unboxing video banayein aur 48 ghante ke andar WhatsApp par bhejein. Sealed aur unused product 7 din ke andar replace ho jaata hai. Details return policy me hain.

Kheti ki salah chahiye?

Apni fasal, mitti aur problem batayein — hamari team WhatsApp par free guidance degi.
