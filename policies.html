Shipping & delivery

Har order pack hone se pehle check hota hai. Working day par order milne ke 24 ghante ke andar hum WhatsApp ya phone par confirmation bhejte hain. Confirmation ke baad hi order dispatch hota hai.

Delivery time

  • Madhya Pradesh ke zyadatar pincodes: 2–5 working days.
  • Baaki rajyon ke serviceable pincodes: 4–7 working days.
  • Remote ya pahadi ilaake: 7–10 working days tak lag sakte hain.
  • Season ke peak par (sowing se pehle) thodi zyada der ho sakti hai — hum pehle se batayenge.

Delivery charges

  • ₹999 ya usse zyada ke order par delivery free hai.
  • ₹999 se kam ke order par ₹49 delivery charge lagta hai.
  • Cash on Delivery chunne par ₹30 handling fee alag se jodi jaati hai.
  • Cart aur checkout page par final amount hamesha dikhaya jaata hai — koi chhupa charge nahi.

Pincode aur tracking

Kuch pincodes par bhaari khad ya sprayer ki delivery available nahi hoti. Aise case me hum order confirm karne se pehle aapko batate hain aur poora amount refund kar dete hain. Dispatch hone par tracking details WhatsApp par bheji jaati hain.

Delivery ke waqt

  • Delivery partner 2 baar contact karega. Dono baar na milne par parcel warehouse wapas aa jaata hai.
  • Parcel phaTa, geela ya khula lage to use receive na karein aur turant humein WhatsApp karein.
  • Unboxing ka video banayein — damaged ya galat product ke claim ke liye yeh zaroori hai.
  • Cash on Delivery me payment product receive karte waqt hi karein. Advance me kisi personal number par paisa na bhejein.

Return & replacement policy

Hamara maksad simple hai — sahi product aapke khet tak pahunche. Fir bhi kuch gadbad ho jaye to hum replacement ya refund denge, niche diye gaye niyamon ke hisaab se.

Kab tak report karein

  • Damage, leak, missing item ya galat product: delivery ke 48 ghante ke andar.
  • Expired ya galat batch: delivery ke 7 din ke andar.
  • Seed germination issue: sowing ke 15 din ke andar, batch number ke saath.

Kya return ho sakta hai

  • Sealed, unused product jiska packing original ho.
  • Damaged, leaked ya broken product (photo/video proof ke saath).
  • Galat ya adhoora bheja gaya order.
  • Expired product ya aisa product jiska label galat ho.

Kya return nahi ho sakta

  • Khula hua pesticide, fungicide ya fertilizer — safety aur storage reasons se.
  • Beej ka khula packet, ya jab beej bo diye ja chuke hain (germination claim alag se dekha jaata hai).
  • Use ho chuke sprayer ya tools, aur saaf-safai ke baad bhi damaged product.
  • 48 ghante ke baad report ki gayi damage, ya bina unboxing video ke claim.
  • Sale/clearance items — in par sirf manufacturing defect cover hota hai.

Seed germination claims

Certified beej ki germination guarantee mitti, mausam, sowing depth aur paani par bhi depend karti hai. Claim ke liye humein batch number, packing date, sowing ki tareekh, mitti ka type aur kuch photos chahiye hoti hain. Verification ke baad replacement ya credit note diya jaata hai. Galat storage ya zyada paani se kharab hui fasal claim ke under nahi aati.

Process

  1. Order number, product ki photo/video aur problem WhatsApp par bhejein.
  2. Team 24–48 ghante me claim review karti hai aur pickup arrange karti hai.
  3. Product warehouse pahunchne aur check hone ke baad replacement dispatch hota hai.
  4. Refund case me paisa 5–7 working days me original payment method (COD me bank/UPI) me aata hai.

Order cancel karna

Dispatch se pehle order free me cancel ho sakta hai — WhatsApp par order number bhejein. Dispatch ke baad cancellation return process ke through hoga. Refund amount me delivery charge (agar lagu ho) kati ja sakti hai.

Privacy policy

Hum aapki personal jaankari ka respect karte hain. Yeh policy batati hai ki kaunsi jaankari li jaati hai, kyun li jaati hai, aur aapke paas kya control hai.

Kaunsi jaankari

  • Naam, mobile number, email (agar aap dein) aur delivery ka poora pata.
  • Order details — products, quantity, amount, payment method aur order note.
  • Contact form ya WhatsApp par aapki bheji gayi jaankari.

Kyun li jaati hai

  • Order confirm karne, pack karne aur delivery karane ke liye.
  • Tracking, replacement aur customer support ke liye.
  • Cash on Delivery me delivery partner ko address dene ke liye.

Data kahan rehta hai

Yeh ek static website hai. Cart, coupon aur order ki jaankari aapke browser ke local storage me rehti hai — hamare paas koi server database nahi hai. Aap browser ka data clear karte hain to yeh jaankari bhi delete ho jaati hai. Order WhatsApp par bhejne par hi woh message hamare paas pahunchta hai.

Site par koi third-party analytics, tracking pixel, advertising script ya cookie nahi hai. Koi bhi bahari website se font ya script load nahi hota.

Payment jaankari

Is site par card number, CVV, UPI PIN ya bank password kabhi nahi maanga jaata. Payment delivery ke waqt cash ya delivery partner ke UPI se hota hai. Kisi bhi call par OTP ya PIN kisi ko na batayein.

Kiske saath share hota hai

  • Delivery partner — sirf naam, phone aur address, parcel pahunchane ke liye.
  • WhatsApp — sirf jab aap khud message bhejne ka button dabate hain.
  • Kanoon ke hisaab se zaroori ho to sambandhit authority ke saath.

Hum aapki jaankari kisi ko bechte nahi hain aur marketing ke liye kirae par nahi dete.

Aapke adhikaar

Aap apni jaankari dekhna, theek karana ya delete karana chahte hain to contact page se ya WhatsApp par likhein. Hum 7 working days me jawab denge. Local storage wala data aap khud browser settings se kabhi bhi delete kar sakte hain.

Bachche

Yeh site 18 saal se badi umar ke users ke liye hai. Hum jaan-boojhkar bachchon ki jaankari collect nahi karte. Kisi bachche ne order kiya ho to humein batayein, hum data delete kar denge.

Order aur payment
